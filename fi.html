THE PINNACLE OF MOTORSPORT

Formula 1 is the highest class of international racing for open-wheel single-seater formula racing cars sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). The F1 World Championship has been the premier form of racing since its inaugural season in 1950.

It is a global spectacle, with races held across five continents, attracting millions of fans worldwide. The sport is defined by cutting-edge technology, incredible athletic prowess, and intense rivalries.

F1 AT A GLANCE

Speed Demons

F1 cars can reach speeds of over 370 km/h (230 mph), making them some of the fastest circuit-racing cars in the world.

Aerodynamic Marvels

Downforce generated by F1 cars at high speeds is so immense that they could theoretically drive upside down.

Pit Stop Perfection

A perfectly executed pit stop can be completed in less than two seconds, a testament to teamwork and precision.
